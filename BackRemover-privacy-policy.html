BackRemover Privacy Policy / BackRemover 隐私政策

Effective October 2, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

Who we are

This policy applies to the Android app BackRemover, developed by Dongfu He. For privacy or support questions, contact ringtone.sky@gmail.com.

Photos and editing

You choose photos using the Android system photo picker. BackRemover processes selected photos and their masks on your device with an on-device AI model. The app does not upload selected photos, masks, drafts, or exports to the developer's servers. It does not request broad access to your photo library.

The app keeps temporary processing files in its private cache and may keep one current editing draft and export history in its private app storage. Saved PNG or JPEG files are written to your device's Pictures/BackRemover folder through Android MediaStore. The app does not add a watermark. Sharing a file opens Android's share sheet; the destination you choose receives that file under its own privacy practices.

Diagnostics and support

This release is free and has no in-app purchases. The app does not receive payment details or require an app account.

The app currently has no advertising SDK, remote analytics service, or developer-operated account. Operational event names, error messages, and processing times may be written to the device's local Android diagnostic log to troubleshoot faults; these logs are not automatically sent to the developer. If you email support, you decide what information to include. The app does not automatically attach your photos or diagnostics.

Retention, deletion, and security

Temporary files may be removed by Android when cache space is needed. You may delete an export history entry in the app; this removes the record and leaves the saved image on your device. Delete saved images in your gallery or file manager. You can remove private drafts and history by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app; doing so does not delete images you saved outside app storage.

Private app files are protected by Android's app sandbox, and the app does not enable Android backup of its private data. No Internet transmission of photos is part of the editing process. We cannot control the security or retention of files you share with another app or service.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the changed app version is released. Questions or requests may be sent to ringtone.sky@gmail.com.

适用范围与联系

本政策适用于 Dongfu He 开发的 Android 应用 BackRemover。隐私或支持问题请联系：ringtone.sky@gmail.com。

照片与编辑

你通过 Android 系统照片选择器主动选取图片。BackRemover 使用设备上的 AI 模型，在本机处理所选图片及蒙版。应用不会将所选图片、蒙版、草稿或导出文件上传至开发者服务器，也不请求访问整个图库的广泛权限。

应用会在私有缓存中保存临时处理文件，并可能在应用私有空间保存一个当前编辑草稿和导出历史。PNG 或 JPEG 成品通过 Android MediaStore 保存到设备的 Pictures/BackRemover 文件夹，不添加水印。分享会打开 Android 系统分享界面；你选择的接收应用会按其自己的隐私规则处理文件。

诊断与支持

本版本免费，没有应用内购买。应用不会获得支付信息，也不要求注册应用账号。

应用目前没有广告 SDK、远程分析服务或开发者运营的账号。为排查问题，操作事件名称、错误信息和处理耗时可能写入设备本地的 Android 诊断日志；应用不会自动将日志发送给开发者。如你主动发送支持邮件，可自行决定提供哪些信息；应用不会自动附上照片或诊断内容。

保存期限、删除与安全

Android 可能在需要空间时清理临时缓存。你可以在应用内删除导出历史记录；此操作只移除记录，不会删除已保存的图片。已保存图片可在图库或文件管理器删除。清除应用存储或卸载应用会移除私有草稿和历史，但不会删除保存在应用私有空间之外的图片。

应用私有文件受 Android 应用沙箱保护，且应用未启用私有数据的 Android 备份。编辑过程不需要通过网络传输照片。你分享给其他应用或服务的文件，其安全性和保存期限由接收方负责。

变更与联系

若应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在发布相应版本前更新本页及生效日期。如有疑问或请求，请发邮件至 ringtone.sky@gmail.com。
