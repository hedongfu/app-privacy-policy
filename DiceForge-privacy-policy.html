DiceForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Overview

DiceForge is an RPG numeric dice utility. Dice features work without an account, advertising, or an internet connection. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand aggregated usage and fix stability issues.

Data kept on your device

Roll history, custom-die names and side counts, and theme, animation, sound, and haptic settings stay on your device to provide app features. Android backup is disabled. You can delete roll history and custom dice in the app, or remove local app data by uninstalling it.

Firebase data

When a network is available, Google Firebase processes app lifecycle and screen use, sessions, app version, approximate location, device/operating-system information, and an app-instance identifier. When a crash occurs, it also processes crash stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata, the Crashlytics installation UUID, Firebase installation ID, and session-quality metrics.

This data is used to analyze feature use and diagnose and fix crashes. We remove the advertising-ID, Ad Services, and install-referrer permissions and do not intentionally send roll history, custom-die names, account IDs, user IDs, Crashlytics custom keys, or custom logs. We do not sell this data. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS/HTTPS. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers begin removal from live and backup systems after 90 days.

Sharing, permissions, and contact

We do not export Firebase data, link it to Google Ads, or share it with other third-party services. Firebase/Google and subprocessors needed to provide Firebase process transmitted data under Firebase terms. DiceForge does not request camera, precise-location, contacts, storage, notification, or other runtime permissions.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact ringtone.sky@gmail.com.

DiceForge 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 5 日

概述

DiceForge 是一款 RPG 数字骰工具。骰子功能可在没有账号、广告或网络连接的情况下使用。我们使用 Firebase Analytics 和 Firebase Crashlytics 了解汇总使用情况并修复稳定性问题。

保留在设备上的数据

投掷历史、自定义骰名称和面数，以及主题、动画、声音和触觉设置仅保存在你的设备上，用于提供应用功能。Android 备份已关闭。你可以在应用内删除历史记录和自定义骰，或通过卸载应用删除本地应用数据。

Firebase 数据

网络可用时，Google 的 Firebase 服务会处理应用生命周期和屏幕使用、会话、应用版本、粗略位置、设备/操作系统信息和应用实例标识符；发生崩溃时，还会处理崩溃堆栈、相关应用状态、设备元数据、Crashlytics 安装 UUID、Firebase 安装 ID 和会话质量指标。

这些数据用于分析功能使用和诊断、修复崩溃。我们已移除广告标识符、Ad Services 和安装引荐权限，不主动发送投掷历史、自定义骰名称、账户 ID、用户 ID、Crashlytics 自定义键或自定义日志，也不出售这些数据。数据通过 TLS/HTTPS 传输。Firebase 文档说明 Crashlytics 的堆栈和关联标识符在 90 天后开始从在线和备份系统删除。

共享、权限与联系

我们不将 Firebase 数据导出、链接到 Google Ads，或与其他第三方服务共享。Firebase/Google 及其为提供服务所需的子处理方会按照 Firebase 服务条款处理这些数据。DiceForge 不申请相机、精确位置、联系人、存储、通知或其他运行时权限。

如有隐私问题或删除请求，请联系 ringtone.sky@gmail.com。
